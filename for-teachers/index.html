Kids Games for Teachers

Kids Games are four free learning games that run in a web browser, the thing you use to visit websites. I'm a dad, and I built them with AI in the evenings for my two daughters, who are three and eight, so they could play together at their own levels. If they would help your class, your club or your school's families, you're very welcome to share them.

Open Kids Games

Set them up and read the story

The step-by-step setup guide shows how to put the games on a school iPad or computer, change the characters and add weekly spellings. It's free to reuse in a newsletter with a credit and a link. If you'd like the story behind them, read why I built free learning games my daughters can play together.

The four games

  • Seek & Find (about 3 to 10, two players together): the older child reads clues and solves times table and division questions while the younger one finds big pictures that are read aloud and counts to three. Neither can finish without the other.
  • Times Table Pegs (about 7 to 11): every times table from 1 × 1 to 12 × 12 plus the matching division. You can pick just the tables being practised, and wrong answers come back later.
  • Paint Splash (about 2 to 5): colour names, shapes and cause and effect, with colours spoken aloud.
  • Spell & Build (about 5 to 11): the national curriculum spelling lists from Year 1 to Year 6, or this week's words, with a spelling test mode that reads 15 or 20 words aloud and marks them at the end. The spelling lists are here to print.

Safety and data, in plain words

  • There are no adverts, no accounts, no logins and nothing to buy.
  • The Kids Games pages use no analytics, no advertising tags and no cookies.
  • Families can type in their children's names and choose how the characters look. That is saved only in the browser on that device, and is never sent to me or anyone else.
  • The games load their font from Google Fonts. That is the only outside service they use.
  • Nobody loses, there are no timers, and each game has a natural place to stop.

You can read the full privacy page, and all of the code is free to read on GitHub under the MIT licence.

What they are not

They are not a replacement for teaching or for reading together, and they haven't been reviewed by teachers. Times Table Pegs is not the official multiplication tables check or a copy of it. They are still screen time, so I'd treat them like any other game: a short, happy session rather than an afternoon.

How to share them

  1. Share this link in a newsletter, on a class page or in a parents' group: garystow.co.uk/kids-games
  2. On a school iPad or computer, open the link and use Add to Home Screen on an iPad, or the install option in Edge or Chrome (the exact steps are on the Kids Games page), so the games open like an app and keep working offline. It's the same free web page underneath.
  3. Two children can play Seek & Find on one keyboard, or with two gamepads.

Want your own version? The whole games pack is free on GitHub, with simple steps to make your own copy, change the words or build new games around your class topic. And if there's a game you'd love to see, tell me about it through the contact page. Depending on how complex it is, I may be able to build it for free.
