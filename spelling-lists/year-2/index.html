Year 2 Common Exception Words

Common exception words are spellings children learn by heart because the usual phonics patterns don't quite work for them, words like because, beautiful and people. Below are the 62 Year 2 words from the national curriculum in England that are in my free spelling game, ready to print or practise.

Schools sometimes teach these in a slightly different order or add their own, so check the list that comes home in the book bag as well.

How to practise them in the game

  1. Open Spell & Build on the computer or tablet your child uses.
  2. Under Which words?, tap Year 2.
  3. Tap Guess & spell. Your child reads a clue, works out the word and spells it, and every word builds part of a picture.
  4. When they feel ready, try Spelling test: 15 words. The game reads each word aloud with a sentence and marks the test at the end, like school.

If school sends home a different set of words this week, choose This week's spellings instead and type them in. They stay on that device and are never sent anywhere.
